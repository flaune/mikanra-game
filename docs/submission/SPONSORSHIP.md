# Mikanra — proposed partner pilot

## Offer

Support an authored Japanese-learning cafe experience where a product or cultural activity belongs naturally in the setting. Suitable categories include tea, coffee, stationery, independent publishing and Japanese cultural programs. These are prospective categories; no company is a current sponsor or endorser.

Two offers can be tested:

1. **Contextual placement:** approved packaging, a menu item or a shop display in an existing scene, with an agreed term and territory. Quote after establishing qualified audience and engagement evidence.
2. **Commissioned experience:** a partner funds a short authored scenario, such as understanding a tea order or preparing for a local cultural event, with an agreed educational objective, production budget and maintenance window. This buys production and licensed use, rather than an unsupported audience-reach promise.

## Pilot deliverables

A written concept and learning objective; original or permission-cleared art; reviewed Japanese copy; an agreed branded scene/item; compatibility and accessibility checks; two review rounds; a clearly identified sponsorship acknowledgment; and a short report limited to metrics actually collected. Define delivery date, term, territory, brand-asset permissions, approval contacts, revision limits, maintenance, cancellation and ownership/license terms in the agreement.

Keep the story and Japanese content under editorial review. Do not require real purchases, reward spending with learning scores, sell player data, or imply that a sponsor proves educational effectiveness. The submission demo contains no unapproved partner trademarks or sponsor commitments. Label sponsored experiences and partner claims clearly; review relevant advertising guidance for the actual format. [FTC native advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/native-advertising-guide-businesses).

## Pricing method

Build a quote from scoped writing/development hours, Japanese review, art/audio, rights clearance, QA, revisions, reporting, maintenance and contingency. Add a margin and define taxes/fees separately. No market sponsorship rate or guaranteed impressions are asserted here. Avoid quoting CPM inventory before reliable audience measurements exist.

## Evidence and reporting

Mikanra currently has no advertising inventory system, analytics backend, paid customers or sponsor commitments documented in this release. First validate player fit and repeat use through a consenting adult research pilot. A commissioned project can be evaluated by delivery and approved learning/content objectives; reach-based placement needs reliable audience evidence.
