# Three-minute demo script

Target duration: 3:00. Use desktop capture at 1440×900 or similar, with readable UI and audible Japanese. A public video up to five minutes is optional. This script uses edited scenes; late-day footage may be recorded from an isolated demonstration save. Do not imply that seven days were completed in three minutes or that the footage demonstrates a human learning study.

| Time | Picture / action | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Title, brief train arrival, enter Cafe Mikan | “Mikanra is a cozy browser game for Japanese beginners. You learn by becoming part of a neighborhood, starting with a job at Cafe Mikan.” |
| 0:20–0:50 | Welcome Aoi, play her Japanese order, select drink and quantity | “Every phrase has a purpose. Listen to a neighbor, work out what they need, and ask for help whenever you need it.” |
| 0:50–1:35 | Grind, tamp, attach, extract and transfer coffee; serve and settle payment | “These are real steps in the cafe's service loop. Understanding Japanese lets you do something for someone, while the physical tasks give the words a context.” |
| 1:35–2:00 | Open notes, replay a word slowly, show help/settings | “Your notebook keeps what you've encountered. Readings and meanings stay available; support gradually fades as you show independent understanding.” |
| 2:00–2:30 | Clearly edited later-day washing, regulars, finale and saved optional check | “Across seven mornings, the same people become familiar. The chapter ends at a reading evening, with an optional check and an open cafe for continued play.” |
| 2:30–3:00 | Return to cafe wide view; show brief business/tool-disclosure slide | “The proposed business is a free introduction followed by paid story packs, with carefully chosen sponsored experiences as an additional model. Bolt built the foundation; I directed substantial further development with Codex, and Rive provides preparation feedback. The next step is validating return play, useful learning and willingness to pay.” |

Record the actual release, use original or licensed audio only, and include a link to the playable build. The screenshots in this pack are real automated captures with deterministic camera placement and isolated saves. Do not call them unaided human playtests.
