## Project name

Mikanra

## One-line description

A cozy café game that helps Japanese beginners practice everyday Japanese by serving customers and building relationships.

## Your business idea, who it is for and the problem it solves

Mikanra is a cozy browser game for adult Japanese beginners who enjoy story-driven games. It addresses the gap between memorizing vocabulary and using Japanese in everyday situations: players run a neighborhood café, understand orders, prepare drinks and build relationships with returning customers. Pronunciation, optional meanings and a personal notebook support them as they practice.

The business idea is a free introductory chapter followed by paid story packs with new places, characters and practical situations. Contextual sponsorships and commissioned cultural or branded chapters are additional revenue opportunities. A proposed $14.99 expanded pack is an unvalidated pricing hypothesis; the prototype has no payment system or confirmed sponsors. The next step is to test repeat play, useful comprehension and willingness to pay with beginners.

Development disclosure: Bolt.new created the initial playable foundation. Substantial subsequent core development and testing used OpenAI Codex. Rive CLI created animated preparation feedback. Other tools and content include React, Three.js, Rapier, Tailwind CSS, Vite, Playwright, OpenJTalk/Mei synthetic pronunciation, FFmpeg, Python scripts and Google Fonts. The promotional teaser uses an isolated GSAP composition and the Outfit font. Vercel hosts the current demo. Full third-party credits and licenses are included in the repository.
