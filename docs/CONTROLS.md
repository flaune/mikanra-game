# Cafe controls and service

Use WASD to walk, mouse/arrows to look, E to interact, J for field notes and Escape to pause during exploration. Existing saves resume with Continue cafe.

1. Talk to Haru by the reading corner, then walk around the right end of the counter to open the cafe at the register.
2. Welcome the customer in Japanese, listen, choose the drink and quantity, and repeat the order.
3. Press E near a preparation tool from the staff side to enter the steady workbench view. Walking and free look stop while you work. Use E or the primary button for the next action; hold E or hold the button for pouring and washing.
4. Coffee: grind beans, prepare the machine, then brew. Tea: fill/heat the kettle, measure the confirmed batch, brew, then pour each cup. Water: hold to fill a clean glass. Clean cups are reserved automatically and completed drinks move to the tray. The quantities come from the confirmed order.
5. Escape or Step away returns to exploration, preserving progress. Bring the tray to the customer and offer it with the Japanese phrase. Finish payment at the register.
6. Washing takes one three-second hold for all returned dirty cups. Refill supplies is one action. Both are available from the workbench; no searching for a small pantry target is required.
7. Closing guides you through returning equipment, washing returned cups and wiping the counter. Return to the register to finish the morning.

Heating, extraction and steeping run automatically during active work. The Pause button, hidden tabs and field notes freeze timers and release held input. Leaving a workbench stops a manual pour without losing its partial progress. Ordinary completed drinks always work; holding E during extraction or pouring and releasing in green can earn an optional perfect-cup tip.

The workbench shows the current Japanese preparation word. H reveals its meaning; L plays pronunciation. Field notes retain help and reading settings. Asking for help does not reduce friendship.

Chapter One ends after seven days at the reading evening, with an optional Japanese comprehension check. Keep running the cafe continues to day eight with relationships and learning intact.

Use npm run check and npm run test:e2e for validation. Browser checks include normal WASD navigation from the entrance, guided preparation, later-day management, saved results and continuation. Late-day fixtures remain isolated from the player's browser saves.
