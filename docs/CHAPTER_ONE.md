# Chapter One — running Café Mikan

Chapter One follows seven mornings with Haru, Aoi, Ren and Yui, ending at the neighborhood reading evening. Three visits per morning combine hospitality, physical drink preparation, payments and Japanese. Optional conversations and notebook practice never block the story. The intended pace is about 10–15 minutes per morning; an unaided human timing study has not been performed.

## Working at the counter

Press E near a preparation tool from the staff side to settle at the steady workbench. The next action, confirmed order, Japanese help and progress stay together while walking and free look stop. Escape steps away, preserving unfinished work. Use the Pause button or field notes to freeze timers.

Tea: fill and heat the kettle, measure the confirmed order's remaining one- or two-cup batch, brew, then hold to pour each cup. Water heats in eight active seconds; tea steeps in six. Ready tea waits without a spoilage timer. Coffee: grind one dose, prepare the machine (tamp and attach), then brew. Extraction takes six active seconds. Water: hold to fill a glass. Real available cups are reserved automatically and completed drinks move onto the tray.

Use E or the primary workbench button for each action. Hold E or hold the button for pouring, washing and wiping. Early interruptions preserve manual progress. For a perfect pour, release between 65% and 90%; ordinary completed drinks remain sufficient. Coffee extraction runs automatically, with an optional precision release while working. Perfect cups award ¥25 only after correct service and checkout.

Four individually tracked reusable vessels move through clean, reserved, filled, tray, served and dirty states. Haru cleans all returns on day one, including mistakes. From day two, batch washing cleans all returned dirty cups in one three-second hold. From day three, working stocks carry between mornings and can be refilled freely in one action. Washing and refilling are available directly from the workbench, including while brewing. From day five, one neighbor waits behind the active customer without a patience timer.

Closing guides the player through returning cups and emptying equipment, batch washing, wiping the counter and reviewing the till. The green marker follows the current valid task during exploration. Coffee, tea/water and cleanup have supported physical tools and one compact workbench card. Wrong or excess cups restored from a legacy tray expose a direct return action.

## Japanese through service

Confirm the drink and quantity separately. Welcome, serving and thank-you phrases use Japanese choices, with kana, optional romaji, meanings and bundled audio. The added preparation entries are カップ, 茶葉, お湯 (day one), 注ぐ and 洗う (day two), and できました (day three).

After two guided correct uses on separate visits, familiar English meanings fade. Two independent correct uses or consecutive unassisted notebook recalls also fade romaji. Kana remains. H reveals the current preparation meaning; L replays it. Dictionary help and an Always show Japanese meanings and readings setting remain available.

Actual Japanese errors return the relevant word to practice. Assisted correct uses teach without pretending to be independent recall; physical preparation and payment mistakes do not erase comprehension. Personal interpretations remain optional and tentative.

Day two introduces washing; day three adds stock management; day four reduces guidance as evidence grows; day five adds a waiting neighbor; day six rehearses the reading evening; day seven concludes it. The finale offers an optional three-question comprehension check with persistent independent/assisted results. Its help and outcomes survive reloads. Free play continues into day eight and beyond.

## Persistence and presentation

The existing mikanra.chapter-one.v1 and mikanra.learning.v1 keys remain. Legacy trays migrate into vessel state. Vessel contents are authoritative when restoring trays; malformed equipment is repaired. Timers use active gameplay time and freeze during conversations, notebook use, pauses, hidden tabs and viewport suspension. Stable transitions save immediately; timer snapshots are throttled and flushed on visibility changes.

Three.js renders equipment, liquids, steam, vessels and customer reactions. A script-free Rive asset displays preparation progress and readiness. Canvas Lite and its WASM are shipped locally, loaded lazily, and replaced by static feedback if unavailable. No Rive account or speech API key is required to play.
